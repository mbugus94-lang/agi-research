## Research synthesis

The past-week search returned a runtime-and-verification cluster rather than a single AGI breakthrough:

- **TRAJDEBUG** ([arXiv:2608.06346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06346v1), Aug. 6) traces errors across long-horizon trajectories and keeps resolution status and terminal impact attached to diagnoses. This supports preserving raw evidence and provenance in the repository's review surface.
- **Beyond Top-K** ([arXiv:2608.06305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06305v1), Aug. 6) reports that deterministic lexical search plus structural navigation can outperform dense retrieval on a verified financial-document task, with a replayable audit trail. The local implication is to prefer inspectable evidence operations over opaque aggregation when the evidence boundary matters.
- **HarnessOpt-Bench** ([arXiv:2608.06301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06301v1), Aug. 6) evaluates agent-harness optimization under held-out tests, fixed budgets, and versioned candidates. It is a useful guardrail for this project's self-improvement posture: proposed changes need an independent evaluation boundary and should not be treated as permission to self-apply.
- **Resourced Authority** ([arXiv:2608.06353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06353v1), Aug. 6) models governance through bounded compute authorization and explicitly identifies manipulation of the governing electorate as an open problem. This reinforces keeping operator decisions distinct, auditable, and review-gated.
- **A Taxonomy of Cognitive Capability Gaps** ([arXiv:2608.02553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02553v1), Aug. 3) organizes gaps around persistent state, autonomy, self-monitoring, environment interaction, and adaptation. The project remains strongest on self-monitoring and governance; longitudinal memory evaluation remains a priority.
